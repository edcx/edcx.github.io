Cuarenta is Ecuador's national card game: a 40-card deck, points for caídas and limpias, and a race to forty. It's a game people learn at home rather than from a rulebook, and it's usually played across a table with family.

This is a mobile version for the people who already know how to play. Private tables with a room code, so a family spread across countries can sit down together; Spanish first, English alongside it; and a deliberately quiet design — warm paper, brass, and ink, with the cards as the brightest thing on screen. No pay-to-win, no loot boxes.
